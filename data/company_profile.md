# NovaCart - Company Profile

## About

NovaCart is a premium Indian e-commerce platform specializing in consumer electronics, smart home devices, gaming accessories, and lifestyle gadgets. The company serves customers across India and focuses on fast delivery, secure payments, manufacturer-backed warranties, and reliable customer support.

- Returns accepted within 30 days of delivery.
- Refunds are processed after successful inspection.
- Standard shipping takes 3–7 business days.
- Express shipping is available for selected cities.
- Free shipping is available on orders above ₹999.
- Most electronic products include a 12-month manufacturer warranty.
- Cash on Delivery (COD) is available only for eligible products under ₹20,000.
