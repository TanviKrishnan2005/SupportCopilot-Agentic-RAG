# NovaCart Shipping Policy

## Shipping Coverage

NovaCart delivers products across India. We currently do not support international shipping.

---

## Processing Time

- Orders are processed within **24 hours** after successful payment confirmation.
- Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays are processed on the next business day.

---

## Delivery Time

Estimated delivery timelines are:

- Metro Cities: **2–4 business days**
- Tier-2 Cities: **3–5 business days**
- Remote Areas: **5–7 business days**

Delivery timelines may vary due to weather conditions, public holidays, or courier delays.

---

## Shipping Charges

- Free standard shipping on orders above **₹999**.
- Orders below ₹999 are charged a standard shipping fee of **₹99**.
- Express delivery charges vary depending on the location and product.

---

## Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, NovaCart will send:

- Email confirmation
- SMS notification
- Tracking ID
- Courier partner details

Customers can also track their orders through **My Orders** in their NovaCart account.

---

## Delivery Attempts

Our courier partners will make up to **three delivery attempts**.

If delivery is unsuccessful after three attempts, the package will be returned to NovaCart.

---

## Delayed Shipments

If your shipment is delayed beyond the estimated delivery date, please contact NovaCart Customer Support.

Our support team will coordinate with the courier partner and provide an update within **24 hours**.

---

## Damaged Packages

If the package appears damaged during delivery:

- Do not accept the package if possible.
- Take photographs of the damaged package.
- Contact NovaCart Support immediately.

---

## Incorrect Shipping Address

Customers are responsible for providing the correct shipping address.

Address changes are allowed only before the order has been shipped.

---

## Important Notes

- Shipping timelines are estimates and not guaranteed.
- NovaCart is not responsible for delays caused by natural disasters, strikes, or government restrictions.
- Customers will receive notifications for every major shipping status update.
