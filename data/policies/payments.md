# NovaCart Payment Policy

## Accepted Payment Methods

NovaCart accepts the following payment methods:

- Credit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express)
- Debit Cards
- UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM)
- Net Banking
- Wallets
- Cash on Delivery (COD)
- EMI (Available for eligible products)

---

## Cash on Delivery (COD)

Cash on Delivery is available for selected products with the following conditions:

- Order value must not exceed ₹20,000.
- COD availability depends on the delivery location.
- Certain high-value or customized products are not eligible for COD.

---

## Payment Security

All online payments are processed through secure PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways.

NovaCart does not store customers' card details or CVV information.

All payment transactions are encrypted using industry-standard security protocols.

---

## Payment Confirmation

After successful payment:

- Customers receive an email confirmation.
- An SMS confirmation is sent to the registered mobile number.
- The order status changes to **Confirmed** in the NovaCart account.

---

## Failed Payments

If a payment fails:

- No order is created until payment is successful.
- The amount, if deducted, is usually reversed automatically by the customer's bank within 3–7 business days.
- Customers should contact their bank if the refund is delayed.

---

## EMI Payments

Eligible products can be purchased using EMI options provided by participating banks and financial partners.

Available EMI plans depend on the customer's bank and card issuer.

---

## Refund Processing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method.

Estimated refund timelines:

- UPI: 2–4 business days
- Debit/Credit Cards: 5–7 business days
- Net Banking: 3–5 business days
- Wallets: 1–3 business days
- COD Orders: Refund processed via bank transfer after collecting customer bank details.

---

## Payment Disputes

Customers who notice unauthorized transactions should contact NovaCart Customer Support immediately.

NovaCart will assist in investigating payment disputes with the payment gateway and banking partners.

---

## Important Notes

- Orders are processed only after successful payment confirmation, except for eligible COD orders.
- Refund timelines may vary depending on the payment provider and banking institution.
