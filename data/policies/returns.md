# NovaCart Returns Policy

## Return Window

Customers may request a return within **30 calendar days** from the date the order is marked as delivered.

---

## Eligible Return Reasons

A return request will be accepted if:

- The customer received a damaged product.
- The customer received the wrong product.
- The product has a manufacturing defect.
- The product does not match its description on the website.
- The customer changed their mind and the product is unused, unopened, and in its original packaging.

---

## Non-Returnable Items

The following products cannot be returned:

- Gift cards
- Digital products or software licenses
- Products damaged due to customer misuse
- Products without original packaging or accessories
- Personal care items after opening
- Customized or engraved products

---

## Return Conditions

To qualify for a return:

- The product must be in its original condition.
- All accessories, manuals, warranty cards, and invoices must be included.
- The product should not have any physical damage caused after delivery.
- The serial number must match NovaCart's purchase records.

---

## Return Process

1. Log in to your NovaCart account.
2. Navigate to **My Orders**.
3. Select the order you want to return.
4. Click **Request Return**.
5. Choose a return reason.
6. Submit photographs if the item is damaged.
7. Wait for return approval.
8. Pack the item securely for pickup.

---

## Return Inspection

After NovaCart receives the returned product, it will be inspected by our quality team.

Inspection usually takes **2–3 business days**.

If approved, the refund process begins immediately.

---

## Return Shipping

NovaCart provides free return pickup for:

- Wrong product delivered
- Damaged product
- Manufacturing defects

If the customer is returning the product because they changed their mind, a small return shipping fee may be deducted from the refund.

---

## Important Notes

- Returns requested after 30 days will not be accepted.
- Products intentionally damaged by the customer are not eligible.
- NovaCart reserves the right to reject fraudulent return requests.
