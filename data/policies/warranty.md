# NovaCart Warranty Policy

## Warranty Coverage

Most products sold by NovaCart include a manufacturer-backed warranty. Warranty duration depends on the product category and the manufacturer's terms.

---

## Standard Warranty Period

Typical warranty periods are:

- Laptops: 12 Months
- Smartphones: 12 Months
- Smartwatches: 12 Months
- Headphones & Earbuds: 12 Months
- Bluetooth Speakers: 12 Months
- Computer Accessories: 6–12 Months
- Power Banks: 6 Months

The exact warranty period is displayed on the product page and invoice.

---

## What Is Covered

Warranty covers:

- Manufacturing defects
- Hardware failures under normal use
- Factory defects discovered during the warranty period

---

## What Is Not Covered

Warranty does not cover:

- Physical damage
- Water or liquid damage
- Fire or electrical surge damage
- Accidental drops
- Unauthorized repairs or modifications
- Damage caused by misuse or negligence
- Normal wear and tear

---

## Warranty Claim Process

To submit a warranty claim:

1. Contact NovaCart Customer Support.
2. Provide your Order ID and invoice.
3. Describe the issue in detail.
4. Upload photographs or videos if requested.
5. Follow the instructions provided by the support team.

Some products may require customers to visit an authorized service center.

---

## Repair or Replacement

Depending on the manufacturer's policy, eligible products may be:

- Repaired
- Replaced with the same model
- Replaced with an equivalent model if the original product is discontinued

Refunds are generally not provided under warranty unless approved by the manufacturer.

---

## Warranty Verification

NovaCart verifies warranty eligibility using:

- Order ID
- Invoice
- Product serial number
- Purchase date

Claims without proof of purchase may be rejected.

---

## Important Notes

- Warranty begins from the delivery date.
- Accessories bundled with products may have separate warranty terms.
- Warranty service timelines depend on the manufacturer and service center.
