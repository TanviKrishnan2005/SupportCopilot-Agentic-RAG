# NovaCart Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. How can I track my order?

Log in to your NovaCart account and navigate to **My Orders**. Select your order to view the latest shipping status and tracking information.

---

## 2. Can I cancel my order?

Yes. Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped. Once shipped, you can request a return after delivery if the product is eligible.

---

## 3. How long does delivery take?

- Metro cities: 2–4 business days
- Tier-2 cities: 3–5 business days
- Remote locations: 5–7 business days

---

## 4. What payment methods are accepted?

NovaCart accepts:

- Credit Cards
- Debit Cards
- UPI
- Net Banking
- Wallets
- Cash on Delivery (eligible orders)
- EMI for selected products

---

## 5. When will I receive my refund?

Refunds are usually processed within:

- UPI: 2–4 business days
- Cards: 5–7 business days
- Wallets: 1–3 business days
- Net Banking: 3–5 business days

---

## 6. How do I return a product?

Go to **My Orders**, select the product, click **Request Return**, choose the reason, upload supporting photos if required, and submit the request.

---

## 7. What products cannot be returned?

The following items are not eligible for returns:

- Gift cards
- Digital products
- Customized products
- Opened personal care items
- Products damaged due to misuse

---

## 8. What should I do if I receive a damaged product?

Do not use the product. Take photographs of the damage and contact NovaCart Customer Support immediately. If eligible, a replacement or refund will be arranged.

---

## 9. How do I claim warranty?

Contact NovaCart Customer Support with your Order ID and invoice. The support team will verify your warranty eligibility and guide you through the process.

---

## 10. How can I contact customer support?

Email: **support@novacart.com**

Support Hours:

Monday to Saturday

9:00 AM – 8:00 PM IST

---

## 11. Is Cash on Delivery available?

Yes. Cash on Delivery is available for eligible products with an order value below ₹20,000.

---

## 12. How many delivery attempts are made?

Courier partners make up to **three delivery attempts** before returning the package to NovaCart.
